Every car leaves with a file

A written record of the paint we measured, the defects we removed and the film we left on it. No car leaves without one.

01 —

We measure before we touch.

Paint depth in microns, three readings per panel, photographed at 45 degrees under a fixed lamp. That is the first twenty minutes of every booking — before a machine or a bottle comes out of the van.

You get the readings either way. If correction cannot safely remove a defect, we say so and the price comes down.

02 —
Stage 01 — Wash

Wash and decontaminate. Two buckets, iron remover, clay — until the water sheets flat off the panel.

Stage 02 — Correct

Correct and measure. Machine polish, three depth readings a panel, every defect counted on the sheet.

Stage 03 — Coat

Coat and cure. Panel by panel, then forty-eight hours of quiet before the car sees weather.
